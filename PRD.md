# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Project Name

**Student Achievement Management System**

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Students take part in many activities during college.

They may receive achievements in:

* Academics
* Sports
* Cultural activities
* Technical competitions
* Workshops
* Seminars

Students may find it difficult to keep all their achievement details and certificates in one place.

---

## 3. Proposed Solution

The **Student Achievement Management System** is a simple system that helps students store and manage their achievements in one place.

Students can add, view, edit, and delete their achievement details.

---

## 4. Target Users

The main users are:

* College students
* Teachers
* College administrators

---

## 5. Objectives

The system should:

1. Help students record their achievements.
2. Keep achievement details organized.
3. Make achievements easy to find.
4. Help students maintain their achievement history.
5. Reduce the need for maintaining paper records.

---

## 6. Main Features

### 6.1 Add Achievement

Students can add details about a new achievement.

Example:

* Achievement name
* Category
* Date
* Level
* Description

### 6.2 View Achievements

Students can see all their recorded achievements.

### 6.3 Edit Achievement

Students can change incorrect or old achievement details.

### 6.4 Delete Achievement

Students can remove an achievement record when needed.

### 6.5 Search Achievement

Students can search for a particular achievement.

### 6.6 Achievement Categories

The system can organize achievements into:

* Academic
* Sports
* Cultural
* Technical
* Other

### 6.7 Certificate Details

Students can store information about certificates related to their achievements.

---

## 7. Functional Requirements

The system must allow users to:

* Add achievement details.
* View achievement records.
* Edit achievement records.
* Delete achievement records.
* Search achievements.
* Select an achievement category.
* Store certificate information.
* View a student's achievement history.

---

## 8. Non-Functional Requirements

The system should be:

* Easy to use.
* Simple to understand.
* Fast.
* Reliable.
* Secure.
* Easy to maintain.

---

## 9. User Flow

The basic process is:

**Login → Add Achievement → Enter Details → Save → View Achievement**

For example:

**Login → Add Achievement → Technical → Python Competition → Save**

---

## 10. Example Achievement

A student can enter:

**Student Name:** Ishwarya
**Achievement:** Python Coding Competition
**Category:** Technical
**Level:** College
**Date:** September 2026
**Certificate:** Available

The system stores this information for future use.

---

The project can be developed using:

**Database:**

---

## 12. Expected Result

The system should provide one place where students can manage their achievements.

It should make it easy to:

* Add achievements.
* Find achievements.
* Update information.
* View achievement history.
* Maintain achievement records.

---

## 13. Future Scope

In the future, the system can include:

* Online certificate upload.
* Student achievement dashboard.
* Achievement statistics.
* PDF achievement report.
* Mobile application.
* College-level achievement ranking.
* Automatic achievement reminders.

---

## 14. Success Criteria

The project will be successful if students can:

* Easily add achievements.
* Easily view their achievements.
* Search for old achievements.
* Update their information.
* Maintain all achievement details in one place.

---

## 15. Conclusion

The **Student Achievement Management System** is a simple and useful system for college students. It helps students organize their academic and extracurricular achievements in one place. It can save time and make it easier to maintain achievement records.
